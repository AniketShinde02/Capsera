# Getting Started with Capsera

In this guide, you'll learn how to generate your first viral caption.

## Prerequisites

- An internet connection.
- An image (JPEG, PNG, or WebP) under 10MB.
- A modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge).

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Access the Tool
Go to the [Capsera Homepage](https://capsera.online). You don't need to log in to start using the free features.

### 2. Upload Your Image
Click on the cloud icon or drag and drop your image directly onto the page.

> **Note:** We recommend high-quality images. Blurry images may result in less accurate captions.

### 3. Choose your Settings
- **Tone:** Select *Funny* for lighthearted posts, or *Professional* for LinkedIn.
- **Length:** Choose *Short*, *Medium*, or *Long*.

### 4. Copy & Post
Once the results appear, click the "Copy" icon next to your favorite caption.
