# How-to: Generate Captions

Captions are the heart of Capsera. This guide explains the step-by-step process of using our AI to generate high-quality captions.

## The Interface

Our interface is designed for simplicity. You will see:
- **Upload Zone**: Drag & Drop area.
- **Settings Panel**: Mood, Platform, Tone.
- **Output Card**: Where your results appear.

<Callout type="info">
  **Pro Tip:** You can paste an image directly from your clipboard (Ctrl+V) without saving it first!
</Callout>

## Advanced Settings

### Mood vs. Tone
- **Mood**: Refers to the *content* vibe (e.g., "Funny", "Motivational").
- **Tone**: Refers to the *writing style* (e.g., "Casual", "Formal").

### Magic Tags
Capsera uses semantic analysis to find relevant hashtags. We don't just look at the image labels (e.g., "Dog"); we look at the context (e.g., "Best friends", "Park day").

## Post-Generation

Once generated, you can:
- **Copy**: One-click copy to clipboard.
- **Favorite**: Save to your profile for later (Requires Login).
- **Edit**: Tweak the text before copying.

## Rate Limits
- **Guest**: 5 per hour.
- **Free Account**: 50 per hour.
- **Pro**: Unlimited.
