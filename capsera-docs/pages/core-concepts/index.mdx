# Core Concepts

Understand the magic behind Capsera.

## AI Modes

Capsera offers distinct "Personalities" for your captions:

### 😂 Funny
Uses puns, jokes, and Gen-Z humor. Best for memes and casual stories.

### ✨ Aesthetic
Short, lower-case, dreamy vibes. Perfect for Pinterest, VSCO, and "dump" posts.
*Example:* "chasing sunsets. 🌅"

### 👔 Professional
Clean, grammatically perfect, and engaging. Optimized for LinkedIn or business accounts.

## Credits & Limits
Capsera is currently **free to use**. however, to prevent abuse:
- **Rate Limit:** 10 generations per minute.
- **Fair Use:** Scripted/Automated scraping is prohibited and will result in an IP ban.
