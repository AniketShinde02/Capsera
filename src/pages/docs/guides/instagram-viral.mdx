# How to Go Viral on Instagram

<Callout type="warning">
  **Disclaimer:** "Going viral" depends on many factors. Capsera helps with content quality, but timing and luck play a part!
</Callout>

## The Formula

1. **High Quality Visual:** Use natural light.
2. **Hook in the First Line:** Your caption's first sentence must grab attention.
    - *Bad:* "Here is my coffee."
    - *Good:* "You won't believe where I found the best coffee in NYC..."
3. **Use Capsera's 'Viral' Mode:** Select the "Viral" or "Funny" mood. Our AI is tuned to generate hooks.
4. **Call to Action (CTA):** Ask a question. "Coffee or Tea? 👇"

## Hashtag Strategy
Capsera automatically generates 30 hashtags.
- **Don't use all 30.** Pick 5-10 highly relevant ones.
- Mix distinct hashtags (e.g., #SpecialtyCoffee) with broad ones (e.g., #CoffeeLover).
