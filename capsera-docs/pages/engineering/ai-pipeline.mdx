# How Capsera AI Works

Capsera utilizes a multi-stage AI pipeline to deliver the best captions.

## 1. Vision Analysis (Gemini 1.5 Flash)
We use Google's Gemini 1.5 Flash model for its speed and multimodal capabilities.
- **Input:** Base64 encoded image.
- **Prompt:** A highly engineered system prompt that instructs the AI to act as a "Social Media Expert".
- **Safety:** We filter out NSFW content immediately using Google's safety filters + our own text analysis.

## 2. Context Extraction
The AI identifies:
- **Objects**: Phone, Coffee, Laptop.
- **Action**: Working, Coding, relaxing.
- **Sentiment**: Focused, Productive, Zen.

## 3. Caption Generation
Based on the extracted context and your selected **Mood**, the AI writes the caption.
- *Funny Mode*: Injects puns related to the objects. (e.g., "Espresso yourself.")
- *Aesthetic Mode*: Uses lowercase, minimal spacing, and specific emojis. (e.g., "coffee & code. ☕️")

## 4. Optimization
Finally, we append a block of 30 hashtags sorted by relevance and popularity volume (simulated).
