# TikTok SEO for Captions

TikTok relies heavily on text for search. Here is how to optimize.

## Keywords are Key
Capsera's AI identifies key objects in your video/image. Ensure these keywords appear in the caption.

*Example:* If your video is about baking cookies, ensure "baking", "cookies", "recipe", and "dessert" are in the first 2 lines.

## On-Screen Text vs. Captions
We recommend matching your caption keywords with on-screen text for maximum signal to the algorithm.

## Trending Audio
Capsera doesn't suggest audio yet, but pairing our captions with trending audio boosts reach by up to **40%**.
