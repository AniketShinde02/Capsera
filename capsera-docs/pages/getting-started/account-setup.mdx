# Account Setup & Benefits

While Capsera is free to use without an account, creating one unlocks powerful features.

## Why Create an Account?

| Feature | Guest | Registered User |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Generate Captions | ✅ | ✅ |
| Save History | ❌ | ✅ |
| Favorite Captions | ❌ | ✅ |
| Custom Presets | ❌ | ✅ |

## How to Sign Up

1. Click the **"Sign In"** button in the top right corner.
2. Select "Sign up with Google" for instant access.
3. Or, enter your email and create a password.

## Managing Your Profile
Once logged in, go to `Profile > Settings` to update your preferences or manage your connected social accounts.
